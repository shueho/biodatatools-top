FASTA Alignment to Single-Line Consensus (Per-Position Frequency)

Import aligned FASTA sequences (DNA/RNA/protein), compute the per-position letter composition, and convert the alignment into a single annotated consensus sequence for sequence logo plotting or downstream analysis.

Step 1: Import FASTA Alignment
All sequences must be aligned (equal length). Gap characters (-, ., ?, etc.) are treated as a letter and counted as-is. Letters are case-insensitive and counted in uppercase.
Step 2: Preview and Export

After importing an aligned FASTA file, the converted single-line sequence will be displayed here.
