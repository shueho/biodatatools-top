Distance Calculation: Uses the Haversine formula to compute great-circle distances assuming a perfect sphere (mean radius R = 6371.0088 km).
Formula (lat and lon in radians):
a = sin²(Δlat/2) + cos(lat1)*cos(lat2)*sin²(Δlon/2)
d = 2 * R * arcsin(√a)

Note: The Haversine formula is suitable for most geographic analysis scenarios (e.g., population genetics, ecological isolation-by-distance analyses). For high-precision applications (e.g., engineering surveying, precision navigation), consider using the Vincenty formula (which accounts for Earth's ellipsoidal shape) or professional GIS software (e.g., QGIS, ArcGIS).
