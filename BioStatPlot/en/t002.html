Operation: click an internal node, then "Confirm rotate" to reverse its children; links reflow automatically. "Auto align" reorders both trees by label order; "Undo last rotate" reverts the last change.
Depth mismatch: links turn red when the relative depth of a same-named leaf differs by more than the depth tolerance.
Topology mismatch: a branch is judged by whether the leaf set up to that node also exists in the other tree.
Vertical alignment: Adaptive height matches total height to the larger tree; Uniform spacing keeps equal row spacing, top-aligned; Label match puts same-named leaves at the same height when possible.
